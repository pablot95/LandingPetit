Terms of Use

These terms govern the use of the Pet-it app and the petit-app.com website. We wrote them to be easy to understand: if anything is unclear, email us at contacto@petit-app.com.

They apply from September 11, 2026 to new accounts and from September 26, 2026 to accounts that already existed. How we handle your data is explained in the Privacy Policy, which is part of these terms.

1. Who we are

Pet-it is an app for pet owners. It is operated by Pablo Travi, from Argentina ("Pet-it", "we" or "us" in these terms). You can contact us at contacto@petit-app.com, in Spanish or English. That address is also the single point of contact for users and authorities under the European Union Digital Services Act.

2. Acceptance and changes

By creating an account or using Pet-it you accept these terms. If you do not agree, do not use the service.

We may update these terms to reflect changes in the app or in the law. If a change is significant, we will notify you in the app or by email at least 15 days before it takes effect, unless a legal or security change requires us to apply it sooner. If you do not accept the new version, you can delete your account at any time. Changes never apply retroactively.

3. Minimum age and your account

You must be 13 or older to use Pet-it. If the minimum age to use online services without parental permission is higher in your country (for example, 14, 15 or 16 in some European Union countries), your parent or guardian must accept these terms on your behalf.

  • Your account is personal: do not sell, lend or transfer it.
  • The information you provide must be true and up to date.
  • Keep your password safe. If you think someone accessed your account, change it and let us know.
  • We may ask you to confirm your identity or that you own the account when needed to protect you or others.

4. What Pet-it is

Pet-it lets you create profiles for your pets and post as them; share photos, videos (Zoomies) and stories; follow other pets; publish listings for lost, found and adoptable pets; get alerts about what happens nearby; take part in the community; find professionals and review them; and chat with other people.

Pet-it is free and funded by advertising. There are no in-app purchases.

5. Your content and the license you give us

What you post (photos, videos, texts, comments, listings, reviews and messages) remains yours.

So that we can show it, you give us a free, non-exclusive, worldwide license to host, store, copy, adapt (for example, create thumbnails or crops), display and distribute it within Pet-it according to the privacy you choose. We may sublicense it only to the providers that help us run the service. The license ends when you delete the content or your account, except for copies other people have already shared or downloaded and for the technical deletion period explained in the Privacy Policy.

By posting, you confirm that you have the necessary rights to the content and the permission of the people who appear in it. Do not post other people's personal data without their consent.

6. Community rules

Pet-it has zero tolerance for objectionable content and for people who abuse others. You may not post, send or do:

  • Illegal content or content that promotes illegal activities.
  • Animal abuse, cruelty or fights, or content showing animals suffering other than to seek help or report it.
  • Selling, buying, renting or any commercial trade of animals. Adoptions must be free of charge.
  • Scams: asking for money to return a pet, fake listings, misleading rewards or impersonating owners.
  • Harassment, threats, hate speech or discrimination.
  • Nudity, sexual content or graphic violence, and any content that sexualizes or endangers minors.
  • Impersonating another person, brand or organization.
  • Posting other people's personal data without their permission.
  • Spam, chain messages, unauthorized advertising or manipulation of likes, followers or reports.
  • Viruses, malicious code, automated access, bulk data extraction (scraping) or attempts to breach security.
  • Infringing copyrights, trademarks or other third-party rights.
  • Creating accounts to evade a block or a suspension.

7. Lost, found and adoption listings

Pet-it only puts people in touch. We do not verify the identity of those who post, the condition of the animals or the truthfulness of listings, and we are not part of any agreement between users.

  • Whoever publishes a listing is responsible for it being true and up to date (for example, closing it when the pet is found or adopted).
  • If you do not choose to show the address, the listing shows an approximate location (about 100 meters around the point you marked).
  • The contact details you add to a listing (WhatsApp, phone or Instagram) are visible to people using the app.
  • Safety tips: meet in public places, ask for proof that the pet belongs to whoever claims it, and do not pay in advance.
  • Follow your local laws to adopt or give a pet up for adoption. Pet-it is not a party to the adoption.

8. Professionals and reviews

Professional profiles (vet clinics, groomers, dog walkers, daycares, foster homes and others) are created by the people responsible for them, who must hold any licenses the law requires. Pet-it does not check degrees or licenses and is not a party to the services you hire. A profile may be shown as verified when we checked some of its details; that is not a guarantee about the service.

Reviews must reflect a real experience. Pet-it does not verify that reviewers actually used the service: any fake or offensive review can be reported, and we remove it if it breaks these rules.

9. How we moderate content

We use these tools and measures to enforce the rules:

  • Automated image analysis. Every uploaded photo is analyzed with Google Cloud Vision to detect nudity, violence or explicit content. For pet photos, posts and listings, photos where a person is the main subject and no pet appears are also rejected. We do not use facial recognition or face detection. If the analysis rejects a photo, you see it right away and can ask for a review by email.
  • Reports. You can report posts, stories, comments, listings, professional profiles and community posts from the app. When content receives reports from three different people, it is automatically hidden until we review it.
  • Human review. The Pet-it team reviews reports and review requests.
  • Blocking. You can block another person: neither of you will see the other's content or be able to message each other.
  • Measures. Depending on severity and repetition, we may hide or delete content, issue warnings, suspend or delete accounts, and restrict people who repeatedly send clearly unfounded reports.

When we take action on your content or account, we notify you in the app or by email with the reason, whether the decision was automated and how to ask for a human review, unless the law prevents it. To appeal, email contacto@petit-app.com within 6 months: a person reviews it and we reply with the decision.

10. How to report illegal content

Anyone, with or without an account, can tell us about content they believe is illegal by emailing contacto@petit-app.com. So that we can act, include:

  • Why you believe the content is illegal.
  • Where it is: the name of the pet or profile, the date and, if possible, a screenshot.
  • Your name and email (not required for reports of child sexual abuse material).
  • A statement that you act in good faith and that the information is accurate.

We confirm receipt, assess the report diligently and let you know our decision. If content suggests a threat to someone's life or safety, we report it to the authorities.

11. How what you see is ordered

We do not use algorithms that analyze your tastes to decide what to show you. The order follows simple criteria:

  • Following: posts from the pets you follow, newest first.
  • Near you and Explore: first posts within 100 km of the area you chose, then from your country and then from the rest of the world; within each tier, by number of likes and date.
  • Zoomies: popular videos (by likes) or recent ones (by date), depending on the tab you choose.
  • Listings: by distance from your location.

You can change your area at any time.

12. Advertising

Pet-it shows ads from Google AdMob, labeled "Ad". Personalized ads are shown only if you consent, which you can change in Settings → Ad privacy (when available in your country) or in your phone settings. We do not sell your personal data.

13. Messages

Chats are private between their participants. They are not end-to-end encrypted: we only access them when needed to handle a report, comply with a legal obligation or protect the security of the service. If you delete your account, the other person keeps the messages you sent them, with your name replaced by "Deleted account".

The person who posted the content may respond with a counter-notice.

We may suspend or delete an account for serious or repeated breaches of these terms, by order of an authority, or to prevent harm to other people or to the service. 5. What is public and what is not

  • Visible to people using Pet-it: your username and profile photo; your pets' name, species, breed, gender and photo; listings, professional profiles, reviews and community posts.

  • Only for you and the other owners: health records, routines and your settings.
  • Private between participants: chats.
  • Published location: posts show at most an approximate area; listings, an approximate location unless you choose to show the address; professional profiles, the address of the place.

6. Contacts

Only if you tap "Find friends" and grant permission does the app read the emails and phone numbers in your contacts. Since version 19 of the app, it turns them into digital fingerprints (SHA-256 hashes) on your phone and sends only those hashes; earlier versions send the data over an encrypted connection. On the server they are compared in memory with the hashes of existing accounts, we show you who already uses Pet-it, and they are discarded: we do not store your contacts. Your email and phone number (if you added one) are also stored as hashes so your contacts can find you.

7. Automated decisions

  • Image analysis: Google Cloud Vision analyzes each photo when it is uploaded, and the result only decides whether it is accepted. We do not store the result or use facial recognition or face detection. If you believe a photo was rejected by mistake, ask for a human review.
  • Reports: content with three reports is automatically hidden until a person reviews it, and we notify whoever posted it.
  • Feed order: based on proximity, likes and date, not on your tastes.

None of these decisions produces legal effects on you. You can still ask us to have a person review them.

11. What happens when you delete your account

You can delete your account in Settings → Delete account (we ask for your password, or to type DELETE if you sign in with Google or Apple) or by writing to us. Your account stops working immediately and within minutes we erase your user, email, phone number, photo and settings; the pets you are the only owner of, with their posts, photos, videos, stories, health records and routines; your listings, professional profiles and community posts; the comments, likes and reviews you left; your saved posts, blocks and notifications; and your sign-in with email, Google or Apple.

We keep the messages you sent, which the other person still sees with your name replaced by "Deleted account"; pets you share with other owners; and technical records for the periods in the table above. More information on how to delete your account.

12. Your rights and how to exercise them

Wherever you are, you can:

  • Access your data and take it with you: in Settings → Download my data you create a file with your account information.
  • Correct it: from your profile and your pets' profiles.
  • Delete it: by deleting content or your account.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interest and ask us to restrict the use of your data.
  • Withdraw your consent (location, notifications, contacts, personalized ads) from the app or your phone settings, without affecting what was done before.
  • Ask for a person to review an automated decision.

For any other request, email contacto@petit-app.com from your account email. To protect your data we may ask you to confirm your identity. We reply within 30 days (45 days for U.S. residents), a period we may extend where the law allows; if so, we will let you know. We do not charge for handling your requests unless they are manifestly unfounded or excessive.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can file a complaint with the data protection authority of your country. In Argentina it is the Agency for Access to Public Information (argentina.gob.ar/aaip).

17. On-device storage and cookies

If you sign in with email, it stores your password in the phone's secure storage (Keychain or Keystore) to confirm sensitive changes; it is deleted when you sign out.

The petit-app.com website does not use cookies or tracking tools, and serves its fonts and libraries from our own server. Our hosting provider logs technical visit data (such as the IP address) for security.

18. Security

We protect your data with encrypted connections (HTTPS), Google Cloud encryption at rest, authentication on every request, rules that prevent direct access to the database, signed expiring links to display the photos and videos of posts and stories, and restricted access for the team. No system is infallible: if an incident affects your data, we will notify you and the authorities as required by law.
